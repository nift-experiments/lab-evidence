Totals: 6,580 measured samples plus 456 warmups. Prepared smoke covered all 76 jobs; fresh smoke covered 46 startup jobs plus 30 separately recorded work jobs.

Calibration retains 50 probes per cell: process floor 0.710 ms; small-heap PTY 0.849 ms; 128 MiB PTY 0.867 ms. These are disclosed, never subtracted. Synthetic HOME/XDG/ZDOTDIR and allowlisted environments exclude personal dotfiles and credentials. Fresh application files do not flush OS caches. Fresh configured Zsh retains normal Ubuntu completion-cache generation.

Scripting: 188 jobs across 15 complete families, two sizes and seven participants where credible. Ten measured samples plus two warmups per job: 1,880 measurements and 376 warmups. Serial fresh-process execution and rotating order. Every invocation passes exit/output checks; alternate numeric formatting is accepted only by exact finite Decimal equality. Bash and Lua structured-data/other exclusions retain their established scope and accessible dash rendering. No universal language score, overall winner or forced semantic equivalence is claimed.

C supervision measures fork/exec-to-exit without Python/supervisor-launch cost; compiled PTY timing measures forkpty-to-first-prompt with forwarding/query handling. ## Surprise investigation — separate same-node source builds

After official scripting completed, frozen Nift 4.7.2 and 4.8.0 sources were built on the scripting node with the same compiler and native defaults. Five measurements plus two warmups for each version/case, CPU 0, rotating order, unchanged sources/goldens and every-sample correctness. This diagnostic is never pooled with official results. The 4.7.2 source-built binary differs from the prior published release artifact; its SHA and exact source revision are recorded.

| Workload / size | 4.7.2 source median ms | 4.8.0 source median ms |
|---|---|---|
| sanity/noop/small | 3.459 | 2.950 |
| sanity/loops/large | 75.910 | 78.090 |
| arrays-strings-hash/frequency-count/large | 8630.254 | 531.201 |
| arrays-strings-hash/sort-index/large | 1874.129 | 1114.869 |
| structured-data/json-transform/large | 7439.140 | 543.034 |

Frequency counting and JSON transformation remain substantially faster on identical virtual hardware and compiler. This is consistent with the frozen 4.8 scalar-key indexes and prepared collection/object-literal evaluation changes, an interpretation rather than isolated causal attribution to one patch. Large-loop medians are close and ranges overlap, so the official cross-node loop reduction is not evidence of a Nift-version gain. Sort-index improves in this diagnostic but retains a slower 4.8 observation; it was not discarded. The diagnostic’s ten cases all pass; all raw distributions remain available. Other version/hardware/cache/noisy-neighbour contributions cannot be precisely separated by this limited check.

Remaining large sort-index, BFS, function-call and JSON-parse costs are possible follow-up development targets. No optimization or winner-directed benchmark tuning was performed during this campaign.
